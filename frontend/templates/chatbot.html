Terms and Conditions

This chatbot supports the Guidance Office of the School of Computing at Holy Angel University. It provides guidance information and support, not emergency or crisis care.

Your messages may be recorded and summarized for guidance support. If a conversation is flagged for counselor review, its transcript is retained for authorized Guidance Office staff to assess the concern. Information is handled according to Guidance Office procedures, but confidentiality has limits when there is a serious safety concern or disclosure is required by law or school policy.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or trusted school personnel right away.
